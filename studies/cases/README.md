# Test cases

Heritage places on which *InSites* is tested. Each case has its own folder, with a short description of the place, why it serves as a test case, and the material behind it.

*InSites* is an AI environment for context-based significance assessment (CBSA) of heritage places: a language model carries out the assessment stage by stage, and an expert reviews every stage. What it is and how it works are set out on the [front page of this repository](../../README.md) and in [`docs/`](../../docs/README.md).

| Case | Place | What the folder holds | Language of the source |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Ayelet HaShachar water tower](ayelet-water-tower/) | A 1923 water tower at Kibbutz Ayelet HaShachar, Israel | The documentation file of the tower, with and without its assessment chapters, and the reference to the published assessment | Hebrew |
| [Ard al-Yahud, Haifa](ard-al-yahud/) | A neighbourhood in Haifa's Lower City, read through 28 student survey records | Links to the analysis of the records as a collection, and to its knowledge graph, a network of the places, contexts and values the records name | Hebrew; the Knowledge Graph also in English |

The source documents for practising with *InSites* are in [`../../sites-data/`](../../sites-data/). The current specifications are in [`../../system/`](../../system/). The other research records are listed in the [studies index](../README.md).
