# Test cases

Heritage places on which *InSites* is tested. Each case has its own folder, with a short description of the place, why it serves as a test case, and the material behind it.

| Case | Place | Language of the source |
|---|---|---|
| [Ayelet HaShachar water tower](ayelet-water-tower/) | A 1923 water tower at Kibbutz Ayelet HaShachar, Israel | Hebrew |
| [Ard al-Yahud, Haifa](ard-al-yahud/) | A neighbourhood in Haifa's Lower City, read through 28 student survey records | Hebrew; the Knowledge Graph also in English |

The source documents for practising with *InSites* are in [`../../sites-data/`](../../sites-data/). The current specifications are in [`../../system/`](../../system/).
