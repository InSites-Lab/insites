# Ard al-Yahud, Haifa

## The place

Ard al-Yahud is a neighbourhood in the Lower City of Haifa, on the plain of Wadi Salib. It was founded in 1891 outside the walls of the old city, before the railway station and the modern port, and was later absorbed by the infrastructure and industry that grew around them: a mill, workshops, warehouses, a mosque, a café and a cinema along the Hativat Golani and Kibbutz Galuyot axis. Most of its buildings are now abandoned or partly in use.

## Why it is a test case

Students in a Technion conservation course surveyed the neighbourhood and wrote 28 survey records with a significance assessment, on 25 sites; one record reads the neighbourhood as a whole. The case tests the *InSites* Collection Analysis: reading records together, without producing new assessments, to find the contexts that several places share, and the differences between readings of the same place. Claims and their supporting passages were extracted with AI assistance, checked against the records, and projected onto a Knowledge Graph.
