**A, B and C.** The coding files and the transcript label the same three documents by letter: **A** is the survey, **B** the management report, **C** the regional synthesis. A page reference such as `[C:44]` or `synthesis pp. 40–41` resolves through this table.

**The edition matters.** Every page reference in `../coding/` assumes the pagination of these editions.

**One spelling.** The published citations give **Mordechovich**; the transcript and two coding files, written before publication, spell it *Mordohovich*. Those files are left as they were rather than corrected, because they are a record of what was written at the time. The citations above are the authority.

## The asymmetry, and why it matters for reading the record

The system received the descriptive sections of the manual assessment that the first author had written, and not its significance sections. Anything it produced therefore came from re-processing description she had authored, rather than from information she lacked.
