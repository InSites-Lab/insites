# InSites for DeepSeek

Explore a heritage site's significance through its sources, then use your findings to develop your project. You guide the work and review each stage.

<details>
<summary>中文使用指南（点击展开）</summary>

从资料出发，理解遗产地的文化意义，再用研究发现推进项目。由你引导过程，并逐阶段审阅。

### 1. 准备对话

每个遗产地新建一个对话，上传你收到的 InSites 规范文件（[当前版本](InSites-deepseek-V2.md)）。也可粘贴全文，但上传文件能让对话更清晰。请用你希望使用的语言发出指令；InSites 支持中文和英文，并按你的指令语言工作。

随时可以问：“InSites 是什么？”或“CBSA 方法如何运作？”

### 2. 提供资料并开始

上传文献、照片或图纸，保留来源和页码。输入 **开始评估**，逐行核对八项资料检查表、主要缺口及图片是否可读取。

**评估阶段**

- 0️⃣ **资料初审与缺口** — 核查现有资料及主要缺口。
- 1️⃣ **描述与背景** — 梳理遗产地特征、历史变化与背景。
- 2️⃣ **价值** — 识别遗产价值及其具体载体。
- 3️⃣ **真实性与完整性** — 检视价值如何体现、相关特征保存如何。
- 4️⃣ **比较分析** — 与相关遗产地比较，辨识共性与差异。
- 5️⃣ **文化意义陈述** — 综合说明遗产地为何重要。
- 6️⃣ **质量检查与总结** — 复核评估，归纳发现与局限。

**扩展功能**

评估阶段完成后，可按以下顺序继续探索，使用方法见下文：

- 📖 **解读评估** — 选择一种解读方式，深入探索研究发现。输入 **解读评估**。
- 🕸️ **知识图谱** — 探索地点、人物、事件与价值之间的联系。输入 **知识图谱**。
- 📊 **仪表板** — 查看整体评估和时间线。输入 **仪表板**。

任一阶段均可使用：

- 🖼️ **图像分析** — 分析上传的照片或图纸。输入 **图像分析**。

### 3. 主动审阅

每个阶段的首次输出都是聚焦重点的起点。可用 **展开［主题］** 获取更多细节和更深入的分析，结合资料提出更正，或 **返回第2阶段**。准备好后再说 **继续**；提出问题或更正不等于同意进入下一阶段。

留意第1–6阶段的**反思问题**，借此审视研究发现，并思考它们如何帮助你的项目。

可以问：

- “哪些细节有证据支持？”
- “同样的证据还可以支持什么不同的解读？”

无标记＝资料明确记载；〰️＝根据证据推断；💭＝解读，可商榷。请核对引用；资料中的记载不一定就是已证实的事实。

### 4. 解读评估

每个团队都应在评估后完成至少一种解读。输入 **解读评估**，选择一种解读方式，或提出自己的问题。已有评估也可上传后解读。

> **提示——自己撰写解读请求。** 简要说明你想探索什么、对谁的视角感兴趣，以及这次解读如何帮助项目的下一步。可以调整现有解读方式，也可以提出自己的方式。你的请求将评估与你想创作的内容联系起来。

### 5. 用图谱和仪表板探索

尝试 **知识图谱**，探索地点、人物、事件与价值之间的联系；再用 **仪表板** 查看整体评估和时间线。点击 **Run HTML**（可视化控件为英文）。将图谱中 **AI Query** 的问题复制回对话；每次扩展后，在对话中选择下一步。

### 6. 保存与继续

输入 **保存进度**，获取一份 TXT 进度文件，包含进度摘要、完整的已确认输出及资料清单。下载文件；若只提供文本，请复制并保存为 TXT。保留原始资料；换对话时上传进度文件、同一版本的规范及所需资料。

仪表板是研究发现的可视化报告，可配合使用，但不能替代进度文件或原始资料。

若显示失败，将错误原文发回对话，请求修复显示并保留已确认的内容。

### 7. 分享对话以供项目分析与评价

提交完整的项目对话，包括你们的提问、修正、选择及后续解读。若使用了多个对话，请按顺序提交各自的链接。

在 DeepSeek 网页版中（以下为英文界面按钮名称）：

1. **打开分享菜单** — 点击左侧栏中对话名称旁的 ⋯，选择 Share（分享）。
2. **选择完整对话** — 点击底部的 Select all（全选），核对选中的消息。
3. **创建链接** — 点击 Create public link（创建公开链接），复制生成的链接。
4. **核对内容** — 在新窗口打开链接，确认从最初提问到最后一轮交流均可查看；后续若继续对话，提交前再次核对链接内容。
5. **提交链接** — 按课程要求提交链接。（任何持有链接的人都可查看所分享的内容。）

## 1. Prepare a chat

Start a new chat for each site. Upload the InSites specification file you received ([current version](InSites-deepseek-V2.md)). Pasting its full text also works, but uploading keeps the conversation readable. Write your instructions in your preferred language: InSites works in Chinese or English, following your instruction language.

You can ask “What is InSites?” or “How does CBSA work?” at any time.

## 2. Supply sources and start

Upload documents, photographs or drawings with source and page identifiers. Say **start**. Review all eight checklist rows, key gaps and image readability.

**Assessment stages**

- 0️⃣ **Preliminary review and data gaps** — check available sources and key gaps.
- 1️⃣ **Description and contexts** — trace the site's features, changes and contexts.
- 2️⃣ **Values analysis** — identify heritage values and the features that carry them.
- 3️⃣ **Authenticity and integrity** — examine how values are expressed and features survive.
- 4️⃣ **Comparison with other assets** — identify similarities and differences across related sites.
- 5️⃣ **Cultural significance statement** — bring together why the site matters.
- 6️⃣ **Quality check and summary** — review the assessment, findings and limitations.

**Extensions**

After the assessment stages, explore these extensions in the following order, with guidance below:

- 📖 **Read assessment** — explore the findings through a reading of your choice. Say **read assessment**.
- 🕸️ **Knowledge graph** — explore connections between places, people, events and values. Say **kg**.
- 📊 **Dashboard** — view the overall assessment and timeline. Say **dashboard**.

Available at any stage:

- 🖼️ **Image analysis** — examine an uploaded photograph or drawing. Say **image analysis**.

## 3. Review actively

Each stage's initial output is a focused starting point. Use **expand [topic]** for more detail and deeper analysis, correct a finding using your sources, or **go back to Stage 2**. Say **continue** when ready; a question or correction does not approve advancement.

Notice the **reflection question** in Stages 1–6: use it to examine the findings and consider how they could inform your project.

Try asking:

- “Which details are supported by evidence?”
- “What other interpretation could the same evidence support?”

No mark = stated in sources; 〰️ = inferred from evidence; 💭 = interpretation, open to challenge. Check references: a source's statement is not necessarily an established fact.

## 4. Read the assessment

Every team should follow the assessment with at least one reading. Say **read assessment**, then choose a reading or propose your own question. You can also upload an existing assessment to read.

> **Tip — write your own reading request.** Briefly explain what you want to explore, whose perspective interests you, and how the reading could help your project's next step. You can adapt a suggested reading or propose your own. Your request helps connect the assessment to what you want to create.

## 5. Explore visually

Try **kg** to explore connections between places, people, events and values, then **dashboard** for the overall assessment and timeline. Use **Run HTML** (visual controls are in English). Copy **AI Query** questions from the graph into chat; choose your next step there after each extension.

## 6. Save and resume

Say **save progress** for one TXT progress file containing the progress summary, full approved outputs and source inventory. Download the file; if only text is provided, copy and save it as TXT. Keep original sources; in a new chat, upload the progress file, the same specification version and needed sources.

The dashboard is a visual report of findings. It complements, but does not replace, the progress file or original sources.

If a display fails, paste the exact error into chat and request a repair that preserves approved findings.

## 7. Share the conversation for project analysis and evaluation

Submit the full project conversation, including your questions, corrections, choices and follow-up readings. If you used several chats, submit their links in order.

In the DeepSeek web interface:

1. **Open sharing** — click ⋯ beside the chat name in the left sidebar, then Share.
2. **Select the full conversation** — click Select all at the bottom and review the selected messages.
3. **Create the link** — click Create public link and copy the generated link.
4. **Check the content** — open the link in a new window and confirm it includes the first prompt through the final exchange; if you continue chatting, recheck the shared content before submission.
5. **Submit the link** — submit it according to the course instructions. (Anyone with the link can view the shared content.)
