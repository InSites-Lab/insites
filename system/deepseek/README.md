# InSites for DeepSeek · v11.6

Explore a heritage site's significance through its sources, then use your findings to develop your project. You guide the work and review each stage.

从资料出发，理解遗产地的文化意义，再用研究发现推进项目。由你引导过程，并逐阶段审阅。

### 1. 准备对话

每个遗产地新建一个对话，上传你收到的 InSites 规范文件（[当前版本](V-11.6/InSites-deepseek-v11.6.md)）。也可粘贴全文，但上传文件能让对话更清晰。请用你希望使用的语言发出指令；InSites 支持中文和英文，并按你的指令语言工作。

随时可以问：“InSites 是什么？”或“CBSA 方法如何运作？”

### 2. 提供资料并开始

上传文献、照片或图纸，保留来源和页码。输入 **开始评估**，核对资料、主要缺口及图片是否可读取。

流程：**0 资料初审 → 1 描述与背景 → 2 价值 → 3 真实性与完整性 → 4 比较分析 → 5 文化意义陈述 → 6 质量检查与总结**。

### 3. 主动审阅

每个阶段结束后，可用 **展开［主题］** 深入分析，结合资料提出更正，或 **返回第2阶段**。准备好后再说 **继续**；提出问题或更正不等于同意进入下一阶段。

可以问：

- “哪些细节有证据支持？”
- “同样的证据还可以支持什么不同的解读？”

无标记＝资料明确记载；〰️＝根据证据推断；💭＝解读，可商榷。请核对引用；资料中的记载不一定就是已证实的事实。

### 4. 解读评估

每个团队都应在评估后完成至少一种解读。输入 **解读评估**，选择一种解读方式，或提出自己的问题。已有评估也可上传后解读。

> **提示——自己撰写解读请求。** 简要说明你想探索什么、对谁的视角感兴趣，以及这次解读如何帮助项目的下一步。可以调整现有解读方式，也可以提出自己的方式。你的请求将评估与你想创作的内容联系起来。

### 5. 用图谱和仪表板探索

尝试 **知识图谱**，探索地点、人物、事件与价值之间的联系；再用 **仪表板** 查看整体评估和时间线。点击 **Run HTML**（可视化控件为英文）。将图谱中 **AI Query** 的问题复制回对话；每次扩展后，在对话中选择下一步。

### 6. 保存与继续

输入 **保存进度**。保留进度摘要、完整的已确认输出及资料清单。换对话时重新加载这些内容、规范文件和所需资料。

若显示失败，将错误原文发回对话，请求修复显示并保留已确认的内容。

## 1. Prepare a chat

Start a new chat for each site. Upload the InSites specification file you received ([current version](V-11.6/InSites-deepseek-v11.6.md)). Pasting its full text also works, but uploading keeps the conversation readable. Write your instructions in your preferred language: InSites works in Chinese or English, following your instruction language.

You can ask “What is InSites?” or “How does CBSA work?” at any time.

## 2. Supply sources and start

Upload documents, photographs or drawings with source and page identifiers. Say **start**. Check the sources, key gaps and image readability.

The route: **0 Sources → 1 Description and contexts → 2 Values → 3 Authenticity and integrity → 4 Comparison → 5 Significance → 6 Quality check and summary**.

## 3. Review actively

After each stage, **expand [topic]**, correct a finding using your sources, or **go back to Stage 2**. Say **continue** when ready; a question or correction does not approve advancement.

Try asking:

- “Which details are supported by evidence?”
- “What other interpretation could the same evidence support?”

No mark = stated in sources; 〰️ = inferred from evidence; 💭 = interpretation, open to challenge. Check references: a source's statement is not necessarily an established fact.

## 4. Read the assessment

Every team should follow the assessment with at least one reading. Say **read assessment**, then choose a reading or propose your own question. You can also upload an existing assessment to read.

> **Tip — write your own reading request.** Briefly explain what you want to explore, whose perspective interests you, and how the reading could help your project's next step. You can adapt a suggested reading or propose your own. Your request helps connect the assessment to what you want to create.

## 5. Explore visually

Try **kg** to explore connections between places, people, events and values, then **dashboard** for the overall assessment and timeline. Use **Run HTML** (visual controls are in English). Copy **AI Query** questions from the graph into chat; choose your next step there after each extension.

## 6. Save and resume

Say **save progress**. Keep the progress summary, full approved outputs and source inventory. Reload these, the specification and needed sources in a new chat.

If a display fails, paste the exact error into chat and request a repair that preserves approved findings.
